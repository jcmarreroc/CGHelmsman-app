Updated Physical Standards (No Swim/Row)

Graduation is based on Push-ups (1 min), Plank, and 1.5-mile Run. Swim and 2000m Row are not included.

Training Tips

  • Push-ups: 3×/week, 3–5 sub-max sets; add 1–2 reps weekly; elbows ~45°.
  • Planks: 3–4×/week, 3–4 holds; +10–15s each session; add side planks.
  • Run: 2 easy runs + 1 interval day (e.g., 6×400m @ 5K pace, 1–2 min recovery).
